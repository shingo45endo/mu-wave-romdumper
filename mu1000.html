mu-wave-romdumper

  • MU2000 (4 hours)
  • MU1000 (20 hours)
  • This page reads the wave ROM through the SWP30's registers. It works on the MU1000 and the MU2000, but it is slow: about 20 hours for the whole ROM. An MU2000 should use the MU2000 page instead.
  • Nothing in the synth is changed. The dump only reads the wave ROM.
  • The progress is saved in this browser all the time. You can stop the dump or close the page, and continue later. Do not send anything else to the synth while the dump runs.
